<h3 align="center">Powered by curiosity (and too much coffee).</h3>

I spend most of my time in Go and Python on the unglamorous parts — the queue that must not lose messages, the query that has to stay quick at 3am, the cache key someone will inevitably forget to invalidate. FastAPI and PostgreSQL are where I feel most at home, with Redis quietly holding the seams together. Lately I have been wondering how much of what we call distributed systems is really just careful error handling wearing a fancier hat.
